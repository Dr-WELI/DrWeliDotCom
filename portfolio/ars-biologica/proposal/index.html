Choosing the research, and reading it

Phase one begins with a conversation, and no artistic decision precedes it

I would begin by asking the aquatic ecology team which finding they most want people outside the laboratory to encounter. We would talk about the study site, the problem behind the research and its relevance to water quality, climate pressures or local life. Then we would choose one study, or compatible findings we can present accurately, and discuss what material from the research could be used in the work.

I would read the chosen paper with the authors, asking what the results establish and what they do not. I would look for a human feeling that connects to the finding and could carry a pop song. The example below shows that approach; it is not a decision about the final paper or lyric.

Release of dissolved nutrients from the pond is associated with inflow rates and community recovery, and returns to predisturbance levels before microbial compositions recover. Shabarova, Salcher, Porcal, Znachor, Nedoma, Grossart, Seďa, Hejzlar & Šimek (2021), Recovery of freshwater microbial communities after extreme rain events is mediated by cyclic succession, Nature Microbiology 6, 479-488, quoted from the abstract, p.479. doi.org/10.1038/s41564-020-00852-1

What the finding says. In this forest-pond study, the measured release of dissolved nutrients returned to its earlier level before the microbial community returned to its earlier composition. These are two different aspects of recovery after floods; the paper does not say that all water chemistry returned to normal, nor does it study managed fishponds. I would discuss the wider implications with the authors before using this result in the work.

Something has come back. I am not back yet.

That is a human reading of the finding, and it is never presented as a scientific claim. It gives the working title and one tension that runs through the song, the film and the room at the same time. If the team judges another paper a more urgent starting point for the science and the town, we use that one. A finding about a forest pond will never be presented as a finding about a managed fishpond.

The travel request, said plainly

One arithmetic problem, stated before delivery rather than during it

The call sets the travel requirement at two to five trips to Budweis plus two trips to Ars Electronica, funded up to €3,000. From Sydney, only the bottom of that range is fundable.

I live in Sydney and hold an Italian passport, so I can work and travel in the EU. A return Sydney to Prague fare sits around €1,100 at the low end. Two crossings consume the allowance almost exactly once airport transfers, the Prague to České Budějovice rail and the České Budějovice to Linz rail visits are counted. A stress case with higher fares and unprovided festival lodging reaches roughly €4,000. A third crossing from Australia does not fit under any booking discipline I can apply, and I will not budget as though it does.

So I am proposing the minimum compliant reading of the call's own specification: two stays in Budweis, with both required Ars Electronica visits made by rail from Budweis inside those stays. Everything the call requires still happens, at the bottom of the range it sets.

The call also covers one month of accommodation in České Budějovice from a separate budget. Which of the two stays that applies to needs confirming, because it changes the subsistence arithmetic.

So I ask for two stays: September 2027 and March 2028, the festival month. September stands in for the first scheduled stay. I would arrive with the song finished and mastered, and film while I am there, which puts me in the country at the one moment when the local performers, the scientists and the locations are all available to the same shoot. March 2028 is the festival, when the work goes up.

This needs the organisers' written agreement, because the call describes September as an optional second stay and I am asking for it to stand in for the first. Everything before September runs remotely, and the schedule above is built that way. I would rather settle this at application stage than discover it in delivery.

Decision required before contracting. I request written approval to move the first in-person stay to September 2027. If that is declined, the current two-trip plan must be redesigned with the host; a separate February/March visit, September filming visit and festival visit cannot be represented as covered by the €3,000 allowance.

The installation, in the order you walk it

Six stops across three built stations, so the budget covers what the route promises

A short, accessible route between freestanding translucent fabric surfaces, with positioned speakers so that walking changes what you hear. The shape of the music is experienced physically even when the film is not playing. Every label distinguishes what the scientists observed from what I made of it.

  1. 1 · The fieldworkImages of the work being done, from the research team and from my own time with them. You meet the people and the place before any result. Station one.
  2. 2 · The publicationTheir paper, displayed and readable, credited, in the room. Station one.
  3. 3 · What they sent meAuthorised field or laboratory sounds, photographs, video and documented patterns selected by the researchers, with recordist, study site and scientific limits credited. No environmental sound is labelled as a microorganism's voice. Station one.
  4. 4 · How I workedThe creative process documented: photographs, short videos, my writing, lyric drafts and visual scores, beside the material that informed each one. Printed brochures visitors keep and use, covering the process and what sci pop is, and the different ways research can enter music. Station two.
  5. 5 · The mini cinemaA timed, captioned screening of the finished music film in a controlled listening space. You arrive having already encountered the permitted sources from the chosen study. Station three.
  6. 6 · What happened nextIf the song is released by the festival, the room closes with the release data, the media coverage and the impact it has had. The last thing a visitor sees is the evidence that the work carries on past the gallery door. Station three.

There is no live water, no organism display and no sensor dependency. A Czech exhibition designer and AV technician price, build, test, maintain and strike the route against the real venue, and write the daily operating guide. If the full-run AV quote exceeds the allowance, the route keeps its positioned sound and physical surfaces, the process material goes to print, and one screen alternates between process and film.

Dependencies, and what I am not claiming

The things a delivery assessor should be able to read without digging for them

  • The September substitution needs written approval. The whole two-stay schedule rests on it, and I have set out the Brazil-connected alternative rather than assume it.
  • Full-run AV and the Czech film are the two unquoted high-risk lines. Both carry a named fallback, and both are re-costed in the October to November 2027 written proposal.
  • Scientist participation is proposed and not confirmed. They advise on their research and their material. They are neither unpaid production crew nor presumed co-authors of the song, and they are never assumed to make new recordings without agreement and resourcing.
  • Credits and rights. Everyone engaged is paid and credited, a songwriting credit follows an actual agreed contribution, and a source and rights log runs from the first material exchange. I remain principal artist, creative director, songwriter and performer, and final artistic decisions are mine.
  • Festival lodging, exact festival dates and any required Linz obligations are unconfirmed, and the travel arithmetic above shows both the target case and the stress case rather than only the one that fits.
