Ars Biologica · 2027–28 · working proposal

Flow becomes music, movement and space.

I chose freshwater because flow is a useful artistic texture: it can shape a groove, choreograph bodies and lead an audience through a room. I will anchor that language in research selected with the scientists, then make a pop song that can live beyond the exhibition.

01 · Choose the source

Begin with the scientists' question.

I will ask Dr Shabarova, Dr Chiriac and Dr Dindhoria which findings they want an artist to understand about climate pressure, water quality and local life. We will choose one finding or compatible research whose sites, evidence and limits can be explained honestly.

What is the problem?

Which pressure on freshwater matters here, and which people or ecosystems live with it?

What did you find?

We read papers and discuss the surprising observation, its methods, scale and limits.

What can we work with?

The researchers identify recordings, images and patterns; we check their meaning, rights and credits.

One worked example

Shabarova and colleagues followed microbial communities at a South Bohemian forest pond through two extreme-rain floods. Flooding washed organisms out; the community recovered through succession. A measured nutrient-related signal returned before microbial composition recovered:

“Release of dissolved nutrients from the pond is associated with inflow rates and community recovery, and returns to predisturbance levels before microbial compositions recover.”

Shabarova, T., Salcher, M. M., Porcal, P., Znachor, P., Nedoma, J., Grossart, H.-P., Seďa, J., Hejzlar, J., & Šimek, K. (2021). “Recovery of freshwater microbial communities after extreme rain events is mediated by cyclic succession.” Nature Microbiology, 6, 479–488, abstract p. 479. DOI.

Here the release of dissolved nutrients is what returns to its earlier measured level. A possible human reading is: something has come back, but I am not there yet. I would test that idea with the scientists and as a song about reconnection after disruption. This is an artistic analogy, not a claim that organisms experience longing. Jiřická is not a managed carp pond; another local study would enter only if it adds sound evidence.

02 · Make together

Research changes the work before it is finished.

Scientist selection

Researchers identify existing water, rain, sampling and laboratory sounds, microscopy and documented patterns. They explain why each item matters, what was measured, where it came from and who may license it.

My pop writing

I listen, read and write lyrics, melody and performance. Approved field sound can seed texture or rhythm; an interpretable visual pattern can suggest pacing, movement or an edit. The track must work as pop.

Checks and decisions

I play sketches to scientists for interpretation and to music listeners for musical strength. Scientists review source use and captions. I make final artistic decisions and keep research, analogy and invention distinct.

Production roles. I am the principal artist, songwriter, singer and director. A Sydney producer records, arranges, mixes and masters with me. A Czech movement professional and paid adult dancers develop my movement idea; a Czech filmmaker plans, shoots and edits the music film. A local exhibition designer and AV technician implement and maintain the visitor route. These are proposed, credited specialist roles; no supplier is booked.

Local voices. A 2027 listening and movement lab invites residents connected to regional waters to respond to unfinished sketches. Participation or filming is optional. In 2028 the completed artwork returns for a public conversation with available scientists and performers. I plan twelve captioned short process videos and credited photographs for České Budějovice 2028.

03 · Visitor experience

The installation is a physical sequence.

A freestanding fabric route changes what visitors hear and see as they move. It contains the sources, my translation process and a timed screening. It is a spatial work with a music film inside it.

01 / SOURCE

Listen and observe

Scientist-credited field audio, microscope or site images and short captions identifying the study system.

02 / CHANGE

Compare decisions

Hear source sound beside a song stem and see my lyric, rhythm, movement and storyboard notes.

03 / SONG

Watch together

A timed, captioned screening presents the complete pop film with an accessible route and controlled sound.

A local specialist prices the fabric, equipment and run-length hire after surveying the venue. The working model needs a small process display, film projection and separate audio control. If the full-run AV quote exceeds the allowance, physical source material and one alternating process/film screen retain the sequence. The final technical rider, maintenance guide and safe visitor flow follow the 2027 venue survey.
