From field photography to sci pop

I am WELI, an evolutionary ecologist, songwriter, performer and creative director. My work moved from kangaroo field photography into pop songs, films and public workshops. I chose water for this residency because flow offers a way to compose rhythm, movement and a route through space. The scientists' research gives that artistic language a precise starting point.

Early work · kangaroo photography

My kangaroo photography and photoshoot project began before September 2021, during my doctoral research. It was an early way of making the lives and relationships of the animals I studied visible beyond a data table. My 2021 ANU thesis, Figure 2, reproduces a field photograph credited to me. Field photography and later staged images fed the visual language that I developed further in Kangaroo Time.

The thesis documents a credited photograph by 2021. The project's earlier start and any separate staged shoots should be supported with dated photographs, captions or publication records before relying on them as proof of more than five years of professional creative practice.

Kangaroo Time (Club Edit) · song and music film

I developed my research on eastern grey kangaroo personality and social life into a pop song and film, directing a Canberra cast whose different styles give the work its movement. I co-wrote the song with Nicholas Moynihan and performed in the film; Nic Vevers produced the video. It won the 2024 AAAS Dance Your PhD overall prize.

Cabra da Peste (G.O.A.T.) · commissioned song

For Brazil's Encontro Anual de Etologia 2024, I made a bilingual Latin pop song with Jon Panic from my earlier research on female goat social hierarchy. Cabra names the animal and evokes a person with toughness and charisma in Brazilian Portuguese. That double meaning let the research enter the song through popular culture and a human story, without turning the track into a summary of a paper.

Proposed Ars Biologica work

Flow is why I chose water: it can give a song its groove, guide bodies in a film, and carry visitors from one part of a room to another. I will first talk with Dr Shabarova, Dr Chiriac and Dr Dindhoria about which finding they want me to explore in relation to climate pressure, water quality and local life. Their study systems, images, field sounds and interpreted patterns could shape a song, music film and walk-through artwork. The scientists help identify and interpret the material; I write and direct the art. A local movement professional, paid dancers, a filmmaker and an exhibition/AV specialist bring specialist skills to the production.

One possible starting point is Shabarova and colleagues' study of a South Bohemian forest pond after two floods. The organisms washed out and the microbial community recovered through succession. My possible human starting point is the desire to reconnect after disruption. The final research choice and emotional direction will be made through the residency exchange. A forest pond finding will not be labelled as a finding about managed fishponds.

One artwork, three encounters

01 · EnterResearch in placeField sounds and credited images, with captions saying what scientists observed.
02 · TraceSource becomes songCompare an original sound with a pop stem and see lyric, movement and film decisions.
03 · ListenThe completed filmA timed, captioned screening after moving through the sources.
  1. Encounter the source. Move through freestanding fabric and a controlled listening/viewing area with scientist-credited field sounds, research images and captions that say what was observed.
  2. Hear the transformation. Compare a source recording with a short song stem; see my lyric notes, rhythm decisions, movement score and storyboard beside the material that informed them. Labels distinguish an observation from my artistic interpretation.
  3. Experience the result. Enter a timed, captioned screening of the complete music video. The song also stands as a contemporary pop release beyond the exhibition.

An exhibition designer and AV technician will price, build, test, maintain and remove a safe visitor route with suitable sound and projection. The number of displays and the layout depend on the venue and full-run equipment quote. A simpler single-screen route with physical source material remains the fallback.

See the staged timeline, €15,000 production envelope and €3,000 travel target. These are planning figures; full-duration AV and film costs still need written quotes.
